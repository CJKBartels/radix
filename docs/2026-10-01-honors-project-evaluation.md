# Evaluation for Honors Project

The project will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

1. **Evidence of quality research in the problem — 30%**
   1. Methods for solving various problems
   2. Methods for inputing math equation(s)
2. **Quality of application of research — 20%**
   1. Choices of computer languages
   2. Choices for parsing
   3. Choices in the front end
3. **App works and solves significant problems — 30%**
   1. Polynominals in 1 variable
      1. Exactly
      2. Numerical approximation
   2. Linear Polynomials in multiple variables
   3. Non linear Polynomials in multiple variables
4. **Quality of presentation — 20%**

   Quality of presentation will be determined by the committee. The inputs of each member will be weighted equally. All committee members must agree that the presentation merits a passing grade.

## Grading scale

| Score | Grade |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | A |
| 80–89% | B |
| 70–79% | C |
| 60–69% | D |
